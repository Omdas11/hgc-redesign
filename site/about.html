About the College

Sixty-five years of higher education in the hills of Dima Hasao.

Our Story

From a night college to a hill-district landmark

Haflong Government College was born from the vision of the region's pioneers, who believed the hill district deserved its own centre of higher learning. It opened its doors on 4 September 1961 as a private night college, holding pre-university arts classes for just seventeen students at the Cultural Institute Hall.

Backed by the district's intelligentsia, the young college grew quickly. In 1964 it moved to its present site on College Road, Haflong, where it has stood ever since — articulating the educational aspirations of the district's ethnic communities and carrying learning into one of Assam's most beautiful hill regions.

Today the college offers higher secondary, four-year undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across arts, science and commerce, and remains affiliated to Assam University, Silchar.

Milestones

Key moments

1961

The college is founded on 4 September as a private night college — seventeen students in pre-university arts classes at the Cultural Institute Hall.

1964

The college shifts to its present site on College Road, Haflong, beginning its growth into a full-fledged institution.

Growth years

Arts, science and commerce streams take shape; hostels, laboratories and the central library expand the residential campus.

2019

First NAAC accreditation cycle completed.

2025

NAAC Cycle 2 awards Grade 'B' — a step up that reflects the college's continuing renewal.

2026

In its 66th year, the college looks ahead: digital systems, refreshed infrastructure, and deeper community ties.

What guides us

Vision & Mission

💡

Vision

To be the hill district's most trusted centre of learning — where every student, whatever their background, finds a path to knowledge and livelihood.

🎯

Mission

Honest teaching, accessible education, and all-round development — academic rigour paired with sports, culture, and community service through NSS and NCC.
