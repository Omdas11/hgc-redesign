Admissions

Higher Secondary, Four-Year UG and PG programmes. Merit-based, transparent, and announced on the notice board.

How it works

Admission in four steps

Watch the notice board

Admission schedules, merit lists and counselling dates are published under Notices. Apply only through the announced channels.

Check eligibility

HS entry needs a Class 10 pass; FYUG entry needs HS (10+2) in the relevant stream. Subject requirements vary by department — see the prospectus notice for the session.

Submit your application

Fill the application form within the announced window and keep your documents ready: mark sheets, certificates, caste/PwD certificates where applicable, and photographs.

Merit list & admission

Merit lists are published stream-wise. Selected candidates complete document verification and fee payment on the given dates to confirm their seat.

Good to know

Fees & support

As a government college, fees are kept nominal. Fee tables for HS, UG, PG and hostels are published with every admission cycle.

Scholarships

State and central scholarship schemes are facilitated through the college office — watch notices for deadlines.

Hostel seats

Separate boys' and girls' hostels; allotment follows admission on merit and distance criteria.

Questions about admission?

Write to the college office and we'll point you to the right notice or person.
